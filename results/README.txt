Main results use the selected_* tables and dimensions 5, 4 and 3. R2 is unweighted same-vector reconstruction; weighted_R2 uses inverse squared uncertainty. K is the number of shared directions. q_selected is BH-adjusted across all 1200 primary enrichment tests. The original hallmark_enrichment table includes exploratory top-100/200/300 tests at five directions per representation; its q values use those original families. Residual energy fraction is sum of squared residuals divided by sum of squared observed effects within the gene set, not 1-R2. Bootstrap overlap is the mean squared principal-angle cosine. Manifests count comparisons, not unique participants: do not sum shared controls as independent people. Category assignments are operational groupings. Complete GEO identifiers and group sizes are in the two manifests. No new clinical or causal experiments are implied by these tables.
